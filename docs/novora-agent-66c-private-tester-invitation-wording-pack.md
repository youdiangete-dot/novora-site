# NOVORA Agent 66C Private Tester Invitation Wording Pack

## 1. Purpose

This wording pack gives the NOVORA owner practical, safe language for inviting a
small number of private testers into limited owner-controlled soft-launch
testing.

The goal is to help testers understand the current NOVORA experience without
mistaking private testing for a public launch, a final design process, CAD
approval, pricing, payment, production, shipping, customer delivery, or
automated AI sketch delivery.

Use these templates as starting copy. The owner may adjust tone and details, but
should keep the boundaries intact.

## 2. Allowed use

Use this wording for:

- Private DMs to a small number of personally selected testers.
- One-to-one email invitations to trusted testers.
- Pre-test expectation setting before sending the site link.
- Follow-up messages after a tester submits a Concept Brief.
- Clarifying that NOVORA may manually review the submitted concept direction and
  follow up by email.
- Explaining that any professional CAD, quote, stone availability, production
  feasibility, and final decision steps are separate later conversations.

## 3. Not allowed use

Do not use this wording to:

- Announce a public launch.
- Invite broad public traffic, paid traffic, influencer traffic, or open social
  sharing.
- Promise that every private tester will receive a sketch.
- Promise instant AI generation, automated customer-facing output, or a fixed
  turnaround time.
- Promise final design, CAD, quote, price, stone availability, production,
  shipping, delivery, or order creation.
- Ask testers to submit urgent, sensitive, confidential, or legally restricted
  requests.
- Suggest that a Concept Brief submission is CAD approval, final pricing,
  payment, order creation, production approval, gallery approval, shipping, or
  customer delivery.

## 4. Private tester selection guidance

Choose testers who:

- Understand this is a private test, not a finished public launch.
- Can give calm, specific feedback about the intake flow.
- Are comfortable waiting for manual owner follow-up.
- Will not share the site publicly yet.
- Will not submit urgent, sensitive, confidential, or high-stakes requests.
- Are likely to understand that jewelry design, CAD, pricing, stone sourcing,
  production feasibility, and final approval are separate later steps.

Avoid testers who:

- Need an urgent order or deadline.
- Expect instant AI output or automatic design delivery.
- Are likely to post the site publicly without checking first.
- Need confirmed pricing, sourcing, production, shipping, or delivery now.
- Want to submit confidential personal, business, legal, medical, financial, or
  high-value security-sensitive information during private testing.

## 5. Short DM invitation template

Hi [Tester name], I am privately testing NOVORA with a very small group before
any public launch.

It is a custom jewelry concept intake experience, not a finished ordering,
pricing, CAD, or production system. If you are open to helping, I would love for
you to try the brief flow and tell me where anything feels confusing.

Please do not share the site publicly yet, and please do not submit anything
urgent, sensitive, or confidential during this private test. After you submit,
NOVORA may review your concept direction manually and follow up by email.

## 6. Longer email invitation template

Subject: Private NOVORA test invitation

Hi [Tester name],

I am inviting a small number of trusted people to privately test NOVORA before
any public launch.

NOVORA is currently a guided custom jewelry Concept Brief intake experience. The
private test is meant to check whether the flow is clear, trustworthy, and easy
to complete. It is not a public launch, and it is not an automated ordering,
pricing, CAD, production, or delivery system.

If you choose to test it, please submit only a non-urgent concept direction that
you are comfortable sharing for private testing. Please do not include
sensitive, confidential, or urgent requests.

After a successful submission, NOVORA may review the concept direction manually
and follow up by email. Professional CAD, pricing, stone availability,
production feasibility, and any final design decisions are separate later steps.
A submitted Concept Brief does not mean CAD approval, a quote, a payment, an
order, production approval, shipping, or customer delivery.

Please also keep the link private for now and do not post or forward it publicly.

Thank you for helping me test this carefully.

[NOVORA owner]

## 7. Pre-test expectation message

Before you start, a quick expectation note:

This is a private test of the NOVORA Concept Brief flow, not a full public
launch. The goal is to see whether the intake questions and submission process
are clear.

Please do not submit urgent, sensitive, or confidential requests. Please also do
not assume that a submission creates an order, quote, CAD file, production slot,
or delivery commitment. If the brief is received, NOVORA may review your concept
direction manually and follow up by email.

Professional CAD, pricing, stone availability, production feasibility, and final
decisions are handled separately later.

## 8. Website testing instruction message

When you test the site, please:

1. Start from the design flow link I send you.
2. Move through the guided Concept Brief steps as naturally as you can.
3. Use a realistic but non-sensitive jewelry concept.
4. Submit only if you are comfortable with NOVORA privately reviewing the
   concept direction.
5. Look for the submitted confirmation page after you finish.
6. Tell me if anything feels confusing, unclear, broken, too long, or too
   promising.

Please do not share the link publicly yet. This is still limited private
testing.

## 9. After successful submission follow-up message

Thank you for submitting the Concept Brief.

If you reached the submitted confirmation page, NOVORA received the brief for
private review. I may review the concept direction manually and follow up by
email if there is a useful next step.

For now, please treat this as private testing only. The submission does not
confirm CAD, pricing, stone availability, production feasibility, payment, an
order, shipping, or delivery. Those are separate later steps if the concept is
worth continuing.

## 10. If submission is not confirmed / receipt warning appears

Thank you for testing. If you did not reach the submitted confirmation page, or
if you saw a receipt warning, please tell me before assuming the brief was
received.

During private testing, the safest rule is: no submitted confirmation page means
do not assume NOVORA received the Concept Brief. I may ask you to pause, retry
later, or send me a general description of what happened without sharing
sensitive details.

## 11. If the tester asks about AI sketch timing

NOVORA is not promising an AI sketch for every private test submission.

At this stage, any AI hand-drawn concept sketch work remains internal-only and
requires human review before anything is shared with a customer. If the concept
direction is useful for continued testing, NOVORA may review it manually and
follow up by email.

There is no instant or automated customer-facing sketch delivery in this private
test.

## 12. If the tester asks about CAD, quote, price, or production

The Concept Brief is only the first intake step for a concept direction.

Professional CAD, pricing, stone availability, production feasibility, and final
design decisions are separate later steps. A submitted brief does not approve
CAD, create a quote, confirm a price, take payment, create an order, reserve
materials, approve production, or confirm delivery.

If the concept is worth continuing, NOVORA may follow up manually by email to
discuss what a next step could look like.

## 13. If the tester asks whether they can share the site publicly

Please do not share the site publicly yet.

NOVORA is still in limited owner-controlled private testing, not public launch.
Please do not post the link on social media, forward it to a group, submit it to
directories, use it in paid traffic, or invite people I have not personally
approved.

If you know someone who might be a good tester, please ask me first.

## 14. Internal owner notes for safe communication

- Keep every invitation one-to-one and owner-controlled.
- Use placeholders like [Tester name] and [NOVORA owner] until sending a real
  message.
- Do not include protected admin URLs, full customer references, database IDs,
  system screenshots, logs, headers, cookies, payloads, or provider details in
  tester messages.
- Do not suggest that NOVORA is publicly launched.
- Do not mention paid ads, broad public traffic, or public social posting as
  active plans.
- Do not promise that a tester will receive a sketch.
- Do not promise a response time.
- Do not discuss CAD, quotes, production, sourcing, or delivery as confirmed
  outcomes.
- Keep the main request simple: test the intake flow and share feedback.
- If the site does not show the submitted confirmation page, tell the tester not
  to assume receipt.

## 15. Privacy and data handling reminders

Tell testers only what is needed for private testing:

- Submit only information they are comfortable sharing for private review.
- Do not submit urgent, sensitive, confidential, legally restricted, or
  high-risk requests.
- Do not include private third-party information unless they have permission.
- Do not upload images or references they do not have the right to share.
- NOVORA may review the submitted concept direction manually and follow up by
  email.
- Private testing does not guarantee a sketch, quote, CAD, production, delivery,
  or future service.

Internal owner reminder: do not put real customer email addresses, names,
phones, countries, full public references, database UUIDs, protected admin URLs,
Supabase project URLs, service-role keys, API keys, Resend message IDs, headers,
cookies, or payload data into public docs, public messages, screenshots, or
shared templates.

## 16. Final recommendation

Use NOVORA private testing only with a small, trusted, owner-controlled group.

Do not treat this as public launch readiness. Do not send paid traffic, broad
public traffic, influencer traffic, or open social sharing to the site yet.
Supabase Free project pause remains a major operational risk, and the current
experience still depends on confirmed submission receipt, human review, and
manual email follow-up.

The safest owner message is:

"NOVORA is privately testing the Concept Brief flow. If your submission is
confirmed, NOVORA may review the concept direction manually and follow up by
email. This is not CAD approval, pricing, payment, an order, production, or
delivery, and the site should not be shared publicly yet."
