## 4. Task Admission

Delivery Lock v1 admits future engineering work only against an existing
approved acceptance reference. Each instruction must name:

- The existing acceptance ID, its S1 milestone or applicable operations scope,
  and how the task directly completes or unblocks that outcome.
- A demonstrated blocker and exact source evidence, including the applicable
  S1 allowed-blocker category for corrective work.
- Exactly one bounded execution gate and one acceptance question.
- Permitted files, operations, identity, risk boundaries and authorization.
- The acceptance result, evidence required, authorized budget and stop condition.

Unknown/proposed work cannot become a new release requirement without explicit
Owner scope-change approval. A corrective finding stays within its existing
outcome; renaming or splitting it does not create another milestone or enlarge
the frozen denominator. New scope remains Owner-only. An actual amendment to
final commercial completion additionally requires S1's own change procedure.

S11 remains controlling: WIP=1, no running-task expansion, no conditional
continuation and no automatic next gate. App, SQL, Provider, environment,
Production, customer-data, payment, email and other high-risk actions retain
their existing authorization boundaries. This baseline authorizes none of them.

| Classification | Evidence-based meaning | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Demonstrated critical safety exposure. | Block the affected release; contain only within exact authorized scope. |
| P1 | Demonstrated necessary release blocker against an existing approved outcome and S1 allowed category. | One bounded corrective task; no scope expansion. |

| UNKNOWN | Evidence is insufficient to classify or establish the blocker. | Bounded verification only; no speculative correction or new requirement. |

Default Delivery Lock rework budget: **one diagnosis, one minimal correction,
one targeted validation**. This does not bundle distinct execution gates or
authorize work that the exact task excludes. S11 failure classes, mechanical
correction limits and recovery-chain cap remain in force; the narrower explicit
task budget controls and must not be enlarged through renaming.

Failure or exhausted authorization means **STOP and return OWNER_HOLD**.
Work still reports PASS, BLOCKED or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE as S11 requires;
OWNER_HOLD is the corresponding governance disposition, not a new execution
result or permission to retry. A known mandatory unmitigated risk also remains
a release NO-GO. Exhausting the budget cannot waive a critical safety risk.

## 7. Release And Stop Conditions
